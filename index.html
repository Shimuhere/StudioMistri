03 / A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Good questions.
Clear starting points.

Have something else in mind? Talk to the studio about your project.

Facebook
Do you sell products or offer custom printing?

Both. Explore finished printed pieces on our Facebook page, or contact us about a custom print, fabrication project, or prototype.

What kinds of projects can I bring to you?

We work on student projects, architectural models, prototypes, custom characters, and creative products. Share your requirements so we can discuss what’s possible.

Do I need a finished 3D model?

A model is a useful starting point, but you can also send an idea, sketch, or reference. We’ll discuss the design and preparation your project needs before you proceed.

How do I order or ask for a quote?

Contact Studio Mistri through Facebook or call 01812-868624. For a product, share the post or design you’re interested in. For custom work, include the intended use, dimensions, quantity, and any reference files.

How much will it cost, and when will it be ready?

Ask us for a quote and timing for your specific piece. Share your deadline at the start, and confirm the price, materials, and schedule with the studio before ordering.

Where is Studio Mistri based?

Our Facebook page lists Bashundhara R/A. Contact us to confirm the exact location and discuss collection or delivery arrangements before visiting.
